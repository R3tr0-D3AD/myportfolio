WHO
I AM.

I'm Christian Groenewald — a 21-year-old 3rd-year Business Management student at the University of Pretoria, with a genuine passion for marketing, strategy, and how great brands are built.

What sets me apart is my background: ten years competing in gymnastics, then four years coaching — holding 2 formal coaching certifications. That shaped me in ways a classroom never could — discipline, precision, the ability to perform under real pressure, and the instinct to lead others to their best.

Currently in my third year at UP, I'm actively building real-world experience through web projects and planning to continue to Honours. Every day I'm connecting the dots between athletic mindset and business strategy.

"The same focus that wins competitions builds careers. I'm just applying it to a bigger stage."
